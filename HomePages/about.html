About Me

My name is Caden Boylan and I am a C++ programmer specializing in development
with the Unreal Engine framework. I am passionate about transforming ideas
into engaging, functional gameplay experiences that players can explore and
enjoy.

I enjoy the process of building systems and mechanics that bring game worlds
to life, whether that means implementing gameplay features, solving technical
problems, or refining the player experience. For me, programming is both a
creative and analytical process that allows ideas to evolve from simple
concepts into interactive experiences.

One of my favourite parts of game development is the constant opportunity to
learn. Every new feature or mechanic presents a unique challenge, and I enjoy
pushing myself to experiment, improve my skills, and discover new ways to
solve problems. The process of tackling complex systems and turning them into
something intuitive and enjoyable for players is what keeps me motivated to
continue growing as a developer.
